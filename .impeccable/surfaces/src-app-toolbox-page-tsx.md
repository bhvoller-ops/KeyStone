Scope: `/toolbox` route, full page. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: someone who has already tried courses and videos and is still
stuck; skeptical of hype, needs proof of a working system. Job: decide
whether to start the 14-day trial. Proof: the guarantee, the four named
tools, the founder's real 20-year credential — no invented stats.
Constraint: must stay legible and non-intimidating to a non-technical buyer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Not a course, not a dashboard — a fully-stocked, foam-cut tool chest
where every drawer already holds the exact tool for one job, refusing the
category's neon-SaaS-dashboard default.
OWN-WORLD: engineering red, brushed steel, charcoal foam-cutout shadow
boards, stenciled white labels (Big Shoulders Stencil display, Work Sans
body). No gradients, no glass.
STORY: visitor sees the branded agency already sitting in an open drawer,
reads the guarantee as a riveted warranty card, opens each tool drawer,
meets the founder, claims a drawer at $97/mo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: open rolling tool-chest drawer fills the right half; left
half carries the founding-launch tag, headline, subhead, CTA, and a
drawers-claimed progress bar.
